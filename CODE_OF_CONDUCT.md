# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically constructive project community. Participation should be safe and productive regardless of experience, identity, background, nationality, language, disability, appearance, religion, or personal beliefs.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code with patience, clarity, and respect.
- Give actionable feedback and assume good faith while evidence supports it.
- Welcome questions and explain project conventions without condescension.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, copyright, and security boundaries.
- Accept maintainer decisions and disagree without personal attacks.
- Correct mistakes openly and help restore a productive discussion.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, intimidation, doxxing, deliberate disruption, sustained personal attacks, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable. Security reports, personal documents, credentials, and private information must never be posted publicly.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces. It also applies when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to [kokutbon@gmail.com](mailto:kokutbon@gmail.com). Security vulnerabilities should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). The maintainer may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, issue warnings, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Reports will be reviewed with discretion. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith is prohibited.

## Confidential reporting contact

Email [kokutbon@gmail.com](mailto:kokutbon@gmail.com) with subject `[xTRACT] Conduct report`. Please include relevant links and context and identify any conflict of interest. Request an uninvolved reviewer where possible. KOKUTBON currently has one technical maintainer; independent review is not guaranteed. Do not post sensitive conduct reports publicly or use security advisories for conduct complaints.
